System Overview

24/7 monitoring, high-quality recording and remote access — for safety and peace of mind.

The CCTV Surveillance System is a reliable security solution designed to monitor and protect your home, office, factory, shop and all other premises. It provides 24/7 monitoring, high-quality recording and remote access to ensure safety and peace of mind.

The system captures high quality video from multiple cameras and stores it on a Digital Video Recorder (DVR/NVR). It allows real-time monitoring, playback, backup and remote access.
