1.

System Overview

Fingerprint, face or RFID card — accurate attendance records, without time theft.

The Biometric Attendance System is a smart and reliable solution for workforce attendance management. It uses advanced biometric technology to ensure accurate identification and prevents time theft (buddy punching).

The system captures employee attendance using fingerprint, face recognition or RFID card and stores the data securely. It can work offline as well as online and sync data with attendance management software.

It helps organizations improve productivity, ensure transparency and maintain accurate attendance records.
